## addresses — BO customers addresses version chain

Adds version-specific implementations for the customer addresses pages,
covering PS 1.6.1 through PS 8.2.

### New files
- `src/versions/8.2/pages/BO/customers/addresses/create.ts` — Overrides `pageTitleCreate`
  for the PS 8.x page title format
- `src/versions/1.7.7/pages/BO/customers/addresses/create.ts` — Overrides `saveAddressButton`
  selector (legacy card-footer button)
- `src/versions/1.7.7/pages/BO/customers/addresses/index.ts` — Overrides `successfulCreationMessage`,
  `pageTitle`, and `addNewAddressLink` for legacy PS
- `src/versions/1.7.8/pages/BO/customers/addresses/index.ts` — Overrides `successfulCreationMessage`
- `src/versions/1.6.1/pages/BO/customers/addresses/create.ts` — Full override of selectors and
  `createEditAddress` method for the legacy PS 1.6.x form layout
- `src/versions/1.6.1/pages/BO/customers/addresses/index.ts` — Overrides page title and link selectors

### Modified develop files
- `src/versions/develop/pages/BO/customers/addresses/create.ts` — Added keyboard Tab press after

- `src/versions/develop/pages/BO/customers/addresses/index.ts` — Minor selector adjustments
