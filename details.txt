## orders — BO orders create/index version chain

Adds version-specific implementations for the orders create page,
covering PS 1.6.1 through 1.7.8, and fixes the develop create page.

### New files
- `src/versions/1.7.7/pages/BO/orders/create.ts` — Overrides `createOrder` and
  `setPaymentMethod` for the PS 1.7.7 UI
- `src/versions/1.7.8/pages/BO/orders/create.ts` — Overrides `paymentMethodSelect`
  selector for the PS 1.7.8 summary block
- `src/versions/1.6.1/pages/BO/orders/create.ts` — Full override of customer search
  selectors and checkout history block locators for the legacy PS 1.6.x UI
- `src/versions/1.6.1/pages/BO/orders/index.ts` — Overrides `newOrderPageLink` and
  `ordersParentLink` for legacy navigation

### Modified develop files
- `src/versions/develop/pages/BO/orders/create.ts` — Restored `waitForResponse` call
  after adding a product to cart (was incorrectly commented out)
- `src/versions/develop/pages/BO/orders/index.ts` — Minor adjustments

### Modified proxy files

  (removed empty 8.x passthroughs; fixed missing .boOrderCreatePage on fallback)
